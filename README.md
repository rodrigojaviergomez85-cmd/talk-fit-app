# Fluent Now

Build a mobile-first English speaking training app called:

FLUENCY REPS

The app is designed for adult English learners ages 18–50, primarily Spanish speakers, from beginner to intermediate levels.

It must work beautifully on:

- iPhone

- Android

- Mobile web

The experience must feel modern, premium, simple, motivating and adult.

This is NOT a traditional grammar app.

This is a SPEAKING AUTOMATICITY + FLUENCY TRAINING APP.

====================================================

CORE PRODUCT PHILOSOPHY

====================================================

Students often understand grammar but cannot use it automatically when speaking.

FLUENCY REPS must move students from:

“I understand the grammar.”

to:

“I can use the grammar automatically when I speak.”

The learning sequence is:

HEAR

→ NOTICE

→ COPY

→ REPEAT

→ SHADOW

→ RECORD

→ PERSONALIZE

→ SPEAK

→ GET AI FEEDBACK

→ TRY AGAIN

The student must hear natural English BEFORE producing English.

The purpose is NOT to eliminate the student's accent.

The goals are:

- better rhythm

- clearer pronunciation

- connected speech

- automatic sentence production

- fewer unnatural pauses

- less translation from Spanish

- better grammar automaticity

- more confidence

- more natural English

====================================================

VERSION 1 + VERSION 2

====================================================

Build the architecture so that Version 1 and Version 2 are part of the same product.

VERSION 1:

Guided Fluency Repetition System

VERSION 2:

AI Fluency Coach that analyzes the student's speech and creates personalized repetitions.

For the first working prototype, only use:

SIMPLE PRESENT

Do NOT create multiple grammar courses yet.

====================================================

SIMPLE PRESENT GOAL

====================================================

At the end of the daily session the learner should independently speak for approximately:

35–45 seconds

using approximately:

7–10 sentences.

The final response should include:

- first person Simple Present

- third person Simple Present

- frequency expressions

- he / she

- correct third person -S

- 1–2 uses of BECAUSE

- expanded ideas

- a simple conclusion

- one final future extension such as:

“Tomorrow I am going to…”

Example structure:

I usually wake up at 6:00.

I have breakfast at home.

I start work at 8:00.

I work with customers every day.

My manager works from home.

She usually starts work before me.

I like my job because I learn new things.

Sometimes I feel tired because my days are busy.

Overall, I enjoy my routine.

Tomorrow I am going to start work earlier.

IMPORTANT:

The student should NOT simply memorize this paragraph.

The app teaches the speaking patterns and then pushes the learner to produce a personalized version.

THE 10 DAILY FLUENCY REPS

====================================================

The learner completes 10 reps.

Always show:

REP 1 OF 10

REP 2 OF 10

REP 3 OF 10

...

REP 10 OF 10

The reps are NOT simply repeating the same paragraph 10 times.

They progressively remove support.

The progression is:

LISTEN

→ NOTICE

→ ECHO

→ CHUNKS

→ SLOW SHADOWING

→ NATURAL SHADOWING

→ MODEL RECORDING

→ PERSONALIZE

→ PRACTICE

→ FINAL PERFORMANCE

====================================================

REP 1 — JUST LISTEN

====================================================

Title:

REP 1 OF 10

Instruction:

“Just listen.”

Do NOT initially show the transcript.

Large button:

▶ LISTEN TO MODEL

Play approximately 35–45 seconds of natural conversational English.

The voice should sound:

human

relaxed

natural

conversational

NOT robotic.

After the recording:

LISTEN AGAIN

NEXT REP

====================================================

REP 2 — LISTEN + NOTICE

====================================================

Title:

REP 2 OF 10

Instruction:

“Listen and notice the rhythm.”

Now display the transcript.

Break sentences into meaningful speaking chunks.

Example:

I usually wake up / around six thirty.

I have breakfast / before I start work.

My sister works / from home.

She likes her job / because it's flexible.

Highlight the active chunk while audio plays.

Do NOT highlight words individually.

We want students to perceive LANGUAGE CHUNKS.

====================================================

VERSION 2 — AI FLUENCY COACH

====================================================

This is a critical part of the architecture.

After REP 9, analyze the learner's speech.

Use speech-to-text when connected to an appropriate API.

For the prototype, create realistic mock AI analysis if a speech-analysis API is not configured yet.

The architecture must allow real AI APIs to be connected later.

The AI should analyze FIVE dimensions:

1. GRAMMAR

2. FLUENCY

3. PRONUNCIATION

4. RHYTHM / PAUSES

5. TARGET STRUCTURE

====================================================

1. GRAMMAR ANALYSIS

====================================================

Detect relevant Simple Present errors.

Examples:

“She work every day.”

Correct:

“She works every day.”

“He don't like coffee.”

Correct:

“He doesn't like coffee.”

“I usually goes…”

Correct:

“I usually go…”

Do NOT correct every possible grammar issue.

Prioritize grammar related to TODAY'S TARGET.

For this module:

Simple Present.

====================================================

2. FLUENCY ANALYSIS

====================================================

Estimate:

total speaking time

number of words

words per minute

number of long pauses

number of filler words

sentence completion

speech continuity

Possible feedback:

“You paused several times before finishing your ideas.”

“Try connecting your sentences more smoothly.”

“You spoke for 38 seconds without major pauses.”

====================================================

3. PRONUNCIATION ANALYSIS

====================================================

When technically possible, analyze pronunciation at the word level.

Identify only the MOST IMPORTANT pronunciation opportunities.

Do not show 15 pronunciation mistakes.

Maximum:

1–3 words.

Example:

TODAY'S PRONUNCIATION

usually

works

because

Allow:

▶ HEAR MODEL

🎤 TRY WORD

====================================================

4. RHYTHM + PAUSE ANALYSIS

====================================================

Identify unnatural word-by-word speech.

For example, compare:

I / usually / wake / up / at / six.

versus:

I usually wake up / at six.

Teach the student to group words into natural chunks.

Provide feedback such as:

“Try saying this as one group:”

I usually wake up

instead of:

I / usually / wake / up

Allow immediate practice.

====================================================

5. TARGET STRUCTURE ANALYSIS

====================================================

Automatically check whether the student included:

7–10 sentences

Simple Present

first person

third person

he/she

because

conclusion

Tomorrow I am going to…

Display:

STRUCTURE

8 sentences ✓

Simple Present ✓

He / She ✓

Because ✓

Conclusion ✓

Tomorrow ✓

AI PERSONALIZED MICRO-REP

====================================================

This is one of the most important Version 2 features.

If the AI detects an important weakness, automatically create a short personalized repetition BEFORE the final recording.

Example:

AI detects:

“She work from home.”

Create:

QUICK FIX

Listen:

“She works from home.”

Student hears:

▶ She works from home.

Student records:

🎤 She works from home.

Then change the sentence:

“My sister works from home.”

Then:

“My manager works from home.”

This prevents the student from simply memorizing one corrected sentence.

The system should generate 2–3 variations.

====================================================

DAILY ADAPTATION

====================================================

Future daily exercises should adapt based on student performance.

Example:

If pronunciation is strong but grammar automaticity is weak:

Increase grammar micro-reps.

If grammar is accurate but pauses are excessive:

Increase chunking and shadowing.

If student speaks only 20 seconds:

Train idea expansion.

If student speaks 60 seconds but with many errors:

Prioritize accuracy.

The product should intelligently balance:

FLUENCY

ACCURACY

PRONUNCIATION

AUTOMATICITY

====================================================

MOST IMPORTANT PRODUCT PRINCIPLE

====================================================

FLUENCY REPS should NOT feel like homework.

It should feel like training.

Think:

gym repetitions for spoken English.

The learner repeatedly trains a speaking pattern until the language becomes automatic.

Every session should move the learner through:

INPUT

→ IMITATION

→ CONTROLLED PRACTICE

→ PERSONAL PRODUCTION

→ AI DIAGNOSIS

→ TARGETED CORRECTION

→ FINAL PERFORMANCE

The ultimate goal is not for students to know more grammar.

The ultimate goal is for students to SPEAK THE ENGLISH THEY ALREADY KNOW, naturally and automatically. Build this in phases. First implement the full Version 1 user flow, but structure the code so Version 2 AI features can be added immediately after without rebuilding the app.
